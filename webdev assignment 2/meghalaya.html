Meghalaya - My Favorite Place

Meghalaya is one of the most beautiful places in India. It is famous for its green mountains, beautiful waterfalls, and natural beauty. It is also known as the "Abode of Clouds".

About the Place

Meghalaya is a beautiful state in Northeast India. It is known for its scenic landscapes, caves, waterfalls, living root bridges and rich culture.
Shillong is the capital city of Meghalaya.

Basic Information

Location       : Northeast India
Capital        : Shillong
Best Time      : October to April
Famous For     : Waterfalls, Caves and Living Root Bridges
Popular Place  : Cherrapunji (Sohra)

Major Attractions

  • Nohkalikai Falls
  • Seven Sisters Falls
  • Elephant Falls
  • Living Root Bridges
  • Mawsmai Caves

Activities to Do

  1. Visit beautiful waterfalls
  2. Explore caves
  3. Trek to the Living Root Bridge
  4. Enjoy the scenic mountain views
